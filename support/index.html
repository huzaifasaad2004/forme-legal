Support

Forme help and data controls

For help with your account, purchases, scans or wardrobe, email huzaifasaad2004@gmail.com.

Delete your Forme account

In the app, open Profile → Delete my account. Confirming permanently deletes your Forme account, closet, scans, outfit history and photos. This action cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email Request account deletion

Send the request from the email address on your Forme account. We may ask you to verify ownership before deletion. Do not send identity documents unless support specifically asks through a secure method.

Delete scan data only

Open Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my scan data. This removes scan photos and derived measurements while keeping your account and closet.

Subscriptions

Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel through your device’s App Store or Google Play subscription settings to prevent renewal.

Response

Forme will acknowledge support and privacy requests as soon as reasonably possible and handle applicable privacy requests within the period required by law.
